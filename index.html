The game

What is Spaceblox?

A logic puzzle about colors, played among drifting pixel stars.

Every level starts as a grid with a few tiles already in place, and your job is to fill every empty cell. Each color you place has to fit a small set of rules about rows, columns and neighbours, so every tile you add tells you something about the ones around it. There is no luck and no reflex test: each board can be solved by reasoning alone, and the fun is in seeing why a tile has to go where it goes.

The campaign starts on small yellow-and-blue boards and grows into larger ones with green tiles, purple tiles that hop between linked cells, locked tiles and "=" / "×" links. A guided tutorial introduces each idea on its own before it appears in a real level, and a red highlight shows the moment a placement breaks a rule, so you learn by playing rather than by reading.

It is built for five spare minutes or a whole quiet evening: clear rules, no pressure and no tricks. How the calm pace was designed is in the devlog post Designing a calm puzzle game.

What's in the game

Features

Play and progress

  • Guided, step-by-step tutorial
  • Campaign with easy, medium and hard levels
  • In-game rule guide with valid and invalid examples
  • Hints, unlimited undo and redo, restart and hold-to-clear
  • Three optional star goals per level
  • Mid-level progress is saved, so you can continue later

Achievements and settings

  • Achievements with bronze, silver and gold tiers, plus hidden ones*
  • Dynamic or static space background
  • Separate music and sound-effect settings
  • On Android: optional Google Play Games sign-in for cloud save and achievement sync*

Accessibility and languages

  • Optional colorblind mode: patterns on the tiles and a palette adjusted for better separation, so colors never have to be told apart by color alone*
  • Screen-reader labels for the menus and the game screen*
  • Seven languages: English, Spanish, German, French, Polish, Georgian and Ukrainian

Platforms

  • Android: Google Play, free, contains ads
  • Web: play in your browser on desktop or mobile, in a portrait layout
  • Browser progress is saved on that device only; cloud save is Android-only
  • Built with the Godot engine

* In the browser version now, and coming to Android with version 1.1.0 (see the release notes below).
