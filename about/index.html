About Spaceblox

Spaceblox is a calm color-logic puzzle set in soft pixel space, out on Google Play since 1 September 2026 and playable in the browser. You fill a grid with yellow, blue and green tiles under a handful of simple rules, and purple tiles and "=" / "×" links add twists as the campaign moves from easy to hard. The game is translated into seven languages: English, Spanish, German, French, Polish, Georgian and Ukrainian.

The full rules, beginner tips and FAQ are in the Spaceblox guide, and the devlog goes behind the scenes.

The creator

Spaceblox is made by Giga "gix0n" Sichinava, who designed, programmed and tested it and also built this website. It is made with the open-source Godot engine and its scripting language, GDScript, and is listed on Google Play under gix0n.

How Spaceblox is made

Small, readable changes. Each feature is added in small steps that are tested before they land, and something that does not work is removed rather than piled on.

Puzzles you can trust. Spaceblox has automated logic tests that check puzzle validation and every campaign level, so a level that ships is a level that can be solved.

Clear rules, gentle teaching. Every rule is introduced in a hands-on tutorial and explained again in an in-game rule guide with examples. Mistakes are shown with a red highlight the moment they happen.

Calm pace. No energy meters and no game-over countdowns — a level never fails because time runs out. Optional star goals are there for players who want an extra challenge.

Accessibility. Spaceblox supports optional colorblind tile patterns and screen-reader labels across its menus and game screen (in the browser version now, and on Android from the upcoming 1.1.0 update), and it is translated into English, Spanish, German, French, Polish, Georgian and Ukrainian.

Respect for privacy. Game progress stays on your device by default. On this website, Google's certified consent tool asks before analytics and advertising cookies are used where consent is required, and you can change your choices at any time from the "Privacy settings" link in the footer. See the Privacy Policy.

How Spaceblox is funded

Spaceblox is free to play. It is supported by advertising (Google AdMob in the Android app and Google AdSense on this website), and by players who leave a rating or tell a friend. Ads are kept away from the middle of a puzzle where possible: full-screen ads only appear between levels, after several wins, and extra hints from an ad are always optional.
