About Spaceblox

Spaceblox is a calm color-logic puzzle for Android and the web. This page is about who makes it, how it is built and tested, and how it is paid for. What the game is and what it includes are on the home page, the rules and tips are in the Guide, and the devlog goes behind the scenes of individual design decisions.

The creator

Spaceblox is made by Giga "gix0n" Sichinava, who designed, programmed and tested it and also built this website. It is made with the open-source Godot engine and its scripting language, GDScript, and is listed on Google Play under gix0n.

How Spaceblox is made

Small, readable changes. Each feature is added in small steps that are tested before they land, and something that does not work is removed rather than piled on.

Puzzles you can trust. Spaceblox has automated logic tests that check puzzle validation and every campaign level, so a level that ships is a level that can be solved.

One project, two platforms. The Android app and the browser version are exported from the same Godot project. The browser build runs directly in the page and keeps your progress in the browser's own storage on that device; the Android app can additionally sync through Google Play Games from version 1.1.0.

Design decisions in the open. Why levels never fail on a timer, why hints reveal a clue instead of filling a cell, and why the colorblind mode exists are explained in the devlog post Designing a calm puzzle game, and the reasoning behind each tile rule in How Spaceblox's color rules work.

Translations. All game text is translated into seven languages. Corrections from native speakers are very welcome on the Contact page.

Privacy

Game progress stays on your device by default, and you do not need an account to play. On this website, Google's certified consent tool asks before analytics and advertising cookies are used where consent is required, and you can change your choices at any time from the "Privacy settings" link in the footer. See the Privacy Policy.

How Spaceblox is funded

Spaceblox is free to play. It is supported by advertising (Google AdMob in the Android app and Google AdSense on this website), and by players who leave a rating or tell a friend. Ads are kept away from the middle of a puzzle where possible: full-screen ads only appear between levels, after several wins, and extra hints from an ad are always optional.

Get in touch

Bug reports, puzzle ideas, translation corrections and press questions are all welcome on the Contact page.
