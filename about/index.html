About geeks0n-byte

geeks0n-byte is a one-person game studio run by Giga Sichinava, an independent developer based in Warsaw, Poland. On Google Play the games are published under the name gix0n. Everything you see on this site — game design, programming, art direction, testing, store pages and this website — is made by one person.

What I make

I make small, calm puzzle games for phones and the web, built with the open-source Godot engine and its scripting language, GDScript.

  • Spaceblox is my first released game, out on Google Play since 1 September 2026: a color-logic puzzle where you fill a grid with yellow, blue and green tiles under a handful of simple rules. It is available on Google Play and in the browser, in seven languages.

Besides games, I also build websites.

How I work

Small, readable changes. Each feature is added in small steps that are tested before they land. I prefer removing something that does not work over piling on more.

Puzzles you can trust. Spaceblox has automated logic tests that check puzzle validation and every campaign level, so a level that ships is a level that can be solved.

Clear rules, gentle teaching. Every rule is introduced in a hands-on tutorial and explained again in an in-game rule guide with examples. Mistakes are shown with a red highlight the moment they happen.

Calm pace. No energy meters and no game-over countdowns — a level never fails because time runs out. Optional star goals are there for players who want an extra challenge.

Accessibility. Spaceblox supports optional colorblind tile patterns and screen-reader labels across its menus and game screen (in the browser version now, and on Android from the upcoming 1.1.0 update), and it is translated into English, Spanish, German, French, Polish, Georgian and Ukrainian.

Respect for privacy. Game progress stays on your device by default. On this website, Google's certified consent tool asks before analytics and advertising cookies are used where consent is required, and you can change your choices at any time from the "Privacy settings" link in the footer. See the Privacy Policy.

How the games are funded

The games are free to play. They are supported by advertising (Google AdMob in the Android app and Google AdSense on this website), and by players who leave a rating or tell a friend. Ads are kept away from the middle of a puzzle where possible: full-screen ads only appear between levels, after several wins, and extra hints from an ad are always optional.
