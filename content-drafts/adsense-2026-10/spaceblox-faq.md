# Spaceblox FAQ

Short answers from the published pages. Play Spaceblox on [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.spaceblox.game) or, from 1.1.0, in the [browser](/spaceblox/play/). The current public version is 1.1.0. The Play launch on 1 September 2026 is v1.0, app only.

## Is Spaceblox free?

Yes. The Android app and the browser version are both free to play. They are supported by ads: Google AdMob in the Android app, and Google AdSense on this website, including ads in the browser game. You can also watch an optional ad to receive three extra hints on a campaign level. That ad is your choice. Leaving a rating, or telling someone about the game, is the other way players support it. The published price is free. The pages and store copy I am using do not describe a paid version or an in-app purchase.

## Where do the ads appear?

Full-screen ads are held back until you have cleared several levels. After that they appear between levels, not in the middle of a puzzle. The extra-hint ad only runs if you choose it. On the website, where the law requires consent, Google's certified consent tool asks before advertising and analytics cookies are used. You can read the details in the [privacy policy](/spaceblox/privacy-policy.html).

## Which platforms is it on?

Android, on [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.spaceblox.game), and, from 1.1.0, a browser build at [/spaceblox/play/](/spaceblox/play/). The browser version is laid out in portrait and runs in a modern browser on a desktop or a phone. v1.0 launched on Google Play on 1 September 2026 and did not include a web build. The browser version was released with 1.1.0 on 5 October 2026. The press page lists that as the current public version. I have not published an iOS version. Cloud save is Android-only, and only from 1.1.0.

## Is there a browser version?

Yes. It was released with version 1.1.0 on 5 October 2026. Get the app on [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.spaceblox.game) first, or play in the browser at [/spaceblox/play/](/spaceblox/play/). v1.0, the 1 September 2026 Play launch, is the app only. The web version keeps progress in that browser. It does not have cloud save, and it does not copy a save onto the phone.

## Do I need an account?

No. You can play without signing in. Campaign progress is stored on your device, or in your browser. I do not run my own servers for game saves.

On Android, version 1.1.0 adds optional Google Play Games sign-in. If you use it, Google stores a cloud copy of your progress, settings, and achievements in your Play Games account. In-progress levels are not uploaded. Signing in is never required to play. Achievements can also sync through Play Games when you are signed in.

## Will browser progress carry over to the Android app?

No. The web version saves in that browser's storage on that device. Cloud sync exists only in the Android app, and only if you sign in to Google Play Games. Clearing site data for geeks0n-byte.github.io deletes the browser save. Uninstalling the app, clearing its storage, or using Reset profile in Options deletes the on-device app save. Play Games cloud data is managed in your Google account or Play Games settings.

## Can I play offline?

The browser build includes an offline page that says the application needs an internet connection to run for the first time. I have not published a separate promise that later browser sessions work with no connection. Saves themselves are local: the app keeps progress on the device, and the browser keeps it in that browser.

Ads use Google's services, so they need a connection when they are shown. Optional Play Games sign-in and cloud save also use Google's services. I have not written a claim that a campaign level you already have on the phone requires a connection to solve.

## Is there a timer? Can I fail a level?

Each campaign level can have a time limit, and that limit only applies to the optional time star. If the clock runs out, the star stays empty. The level does not end, and there is no game-over. You can take as long as you want and still solve the board. There is no energy system.

On boards with purple tiles, that third star can instead ask you to stay under a set number of hops. Extra hops work the same way as extra time. The level remains solvable. The star stays empty. The campaign has no separate score that ticks down while you think. Time and hops only decide that one optional star.

## What are the three stars?

1. **Solved.** Finish a valid board.
2. **No hints used.** Finish without using a hint.
3. **Time or hops.** Finish inside the time limit, or, on some purple boards, within the hop limit.

Stars are optional. One star is enough to clear the level. You can replay it later for the others. Achievements reward milestones, star goals, and a few secret feats, with bronze, silver, and gold tiers, plus hidden ones.

## How do hints work?

A hint reveals a helpful "=" or "×" link. = means they must match and × means they must differ. It does not fill a cell. You still make the placement. Each campaign level gives you a small number of hints, and harder levels give you more. If you run out, you can watch an optional ad for three more.

The no-hint star applies to the attempt where you used help. Replaying without a hint can still earn it.

## What version am I on?

v1.0 is the Google Play launch on 1 September 2026: the color-logic campaign, easy, medium, and hard, on Android only. The current public version is 1.1.0, dated 5 October 2026. If the Android app offers cloud save, colorblind mode, and TalkBack, you are on 1.1.0. The browser version exists only from that same 1.1.0 release. It still has no cloud save.

## What did 1.1.0 add?

On Android, the home page lists achievements, cloud saves, colorblind mode, and TalkBack support. The same version adds the browser build at [/spaceblox/play/](/spaceblox/play/). Achievements use bronze, silver, and gold tiers, plus hidden ones, for milestones, star goals, and a few secret feats. Cloud save is optional Google Play Games sign-in, on Android only. Google stores progress, settings, and achievements. In-progress levels are not uploaded. Colorblind mode adds patterns and a palette adjusted for better separation. TalkBack is the screen-reader support named in that Android update. The browser version released with 1.1.0 has the patterns and the screen-reader labels. The privacy policy also says 1.1.0 adds Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics on Android. v1.0 of the app does not include Firebase, and it does not include the web version. The color rules are the same in v1.0 and in 1.1.0.

## Is every puzzle solvable without guessing?

Campaign levels are designed to be solved by logic, and automated checks cover puzzle validation and every campaign level before it ships. If a board feels like a guess, look again for pairs, sandwiches, and halfway counts, or use a hint. The game highlights broken rules in red while you play. I have not published a level count, so I will not estimate how many boards those checks cover.

## Is there a colorblind mode?

Yes. Colorblind mode is optional. It adds patterns to the tiles and uses a palette adjusted for better separation, so you can tell tiles apart by pattern as well as by color. It is a setting, grouped with language, sound, and background. It is in 1.1.0, on Android and in the browser version released with that update. It is not in the v1.0 Play app. I have not published the exact pattern shapes on this site, so I will not describe them from memory here.

## Does it work with a screen reader?

Screen-reader labels cover the menus and the game screen. The 1.1.0 Android notes list TalkBack support. The browser version released with 1.1.0 has the labels as well. I have not published a list of every control that is labelled, beyond menus and the game screen.

## What languages is it in?

English, Spanish, German, French, Polish, Georgian, and Ukrainian. If you spot a typo or an awkward phrase, tell me the language, the screen, and the corrected text. Language is a setting, stored with sound, background, and colorblind mode.

## Does my progress save in the middle of a level?

Yes. An in-progress level is saved, on the device or in the browser. Closing the game does not discard the board. Cloud save, if you turn it on, uploads progress, settings, and achievements. It does not upload in-progress levels.

## What are the controls?

Tap or click an empty cell to cycle Yellow, then Blue, then Green. Press and hold a filled cell to clear it. In the browser, hold for about a second. Tap or click a purple tile to hop it along its arrow. The top bar has Pause, Restart, How to Play, Hint, Undo, and Redo. I have not published keyboard shortcuts.
