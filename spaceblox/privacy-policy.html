Privacy Policy for Spaceblox

This Privacy Policy describes how Spaceblox (“the App”, “we”, “us”) handles information when you use the Android game Spaceblox published on Google Play.

Contact: use the email address listed on the Spaceblox Google Play store listing.

1. Summary

Spaceblox is a single-player puzzle game. Progress (unlocked levels, stars, settings, and optional in-progress sessions) is stored locally on your device. We do not require an account and we do not operate our own player analytics backend.

The App shows advertisements through Google AdMob. AdMob and related Google advertising services may collect and process device and usage data as described below.

2. Information we store on your device

The App may store locally:

  • Game progress (unlocked levels, star results, achievement unlocks)
  • Settings (language, music/SFX, background mode)
  • Optional saved mid-level session data
  • Internal counters used for ad frequency (for example, wins since the last interstitial)

This data stays on your device unless you clear app storage, uninstall the App, or choose to sync with Google Play Games (see section 6). We do not upload this save data to our own servers.

Local save format (summary): progress is stored in user://progression.cfg (versioned sections for Meta, Progression, Session, Ads, and Achievements). Cloud sync uses a JSON snapshot with progress, settings, achievements, and a timestamp field. Mid-level sessions are not included in cloud snapshots.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Spaceblox uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to show banner, interstitial, and/or rewarded ads. Google and its partners may collect information such as:

  • Advertising ID / device identifiers
  • IP address and general device information
  • Ad interaction and performance data
  • Approximate location derived from IP (depending on region and settings)

This processing is performed by Google under their own terms and policies. See Google’s documentation:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • AdMob & privacy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services

In the European Economic Area (EEA), UK, and similar regions, a consent form (Google User Messaging Platform / UMP) may be shown before personalized ads are requested. You can change privacy choices where the platform provides a privacy options entry point.

4. Children

Spaceblox is a general-audience puzzle game. It is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your country). If you believe a child has provided personal information through advertising services, contact us and we will help address it as appropriate.

5. Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Advertising data may be processed by Google and advertising partners as part of serving ads. We do not share your local game saves with third parties.

6. Google Play Games cloud save (optional)

On Android, you may sign in to Google Play Games and sync campaign progress. When enabled, a JSON snapshot (progress, settings, achievements, and a timestamp) is stored in your Play Games account by Google—not on servers operated by us. Sync merges progress fields (for example, highest unlocked level and star bits) and may ask you to choose between device and cloud copies when timestamps tie. Mid-level session saves are not uploaded.

7. Data retention

  • Local game data: until you clear app data or uninstall.
  • Ad-related data: retained by Google / partners according to their policies.

8. Data deletion

Spaceblox does not create its own player accounts. To delete local game data associated with the App on your device:

  • Open Android Settings → Apps → Spaceblox → Storage → Clear storage / Clear data, or
  • Uninstall Spaceblox (this removes local saves on that device).

If you used optional Google Play Games sign-in, cloud progress is stored in your Google Play Games account. You can manage or delete that data through your Google account / Play Games settings, or contact us via the Spaceblox Google Play listing and we will help with a deletion request to the extent we can.

9. Your choices

  • Reset progress or clear app storage in Android system settings
  • Limit ad tracking in your device advertising / privacy settings
  • Use in-ad or UMP privacy options where available
  • Uninstall the App at any time

10. International users

If you use the App outside your home country, advertising partners may process data in other countries as described in Google’s policies.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.

12. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy: contact via the Google Play store listing for Spaceblox.
