Privacy Policy for Spaceblox

Last updated: September 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Spaceblox (“the App”, “we”, “us”) handles information when you use the Android game Spaceblox published on Google Play, and when you visit our websites on geeks0n-byte.github.io (including /spaceblox/, the browser play page, and other game pages such as Shapes).

Contact: use the email address listed on the Spaceblox Google Play store listing.

What we collect

In plain language: Spaceblox keeps your progress and settings on your device. Ads may use device and usage data through Google AdMob (Android) or AdSense (website). Optional Google Play Games can sync progress, settings, and achievements to your Play Games account. The website uses Google Analytics to understand traffic. We do not upload your game saves to our own servers.

  • On your device: game progress, settings (language, audio, background), optional mid-level sessions, and internal ad-frequency counters.
  • Advertising (AdMob / AdSense): device/ad identifiers, IP and device info, ad interaction data — processed by Google under their policies.
  • Website analytics (Google Analytics): page views and related usage metrics on our site — processed by Google.
  • Optional Play Games: if you sign in, a cloud snapshot of progress, settings, and achievements (mid-level sessions stay local).

1. Summary

Spaceblox is a single-player puzzle game. Progress (unlocked levels, stars, settings, and optional in-progress sessions) is stored locally on your device. We do not require an account and we do not operate our own player-save backend.

The Android App shows advertisements through Google AdMob. The website may show ads through Google AdSense and measures visits with Google Analytics. Google’s services may collect and process device and usage data as described below.

2. Information we store on your device

The App may store locally:

  • Game progress (unlocked levels, star results, achievement unlocks)
  • Settings (language, music/SFX, background mode)
  • Optional saved mid-level session data
  • Internal counters used for ad frequency (for example, wins since the last interstitial)

This data stays on your device unless you clear app storage, uninstall the App, or choose to sync with Google Play Games (see section 7). We do not upload this save data to our own servers.

Local save format (summary): progress is stored in user://progression.cfg (versioned sections for Meta, Progression, Session, Ads, and Achievements). Cloud sync uses a JSON snapshot with progress, settings, achievements, and a timestamp field. Mid-level sessions are not included in cloud snapshots.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Spaceblox uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to show banner, interstitial, and/or rewarded ads. Google and its partners may collect information such as:

  • Advertising ID / device identifiers
  • IP address and general device information
  • Ad interaction and performance data
  • Approximate location derived from IP (depending on region and settings)

This processing is performed by Google under their own terms and policies. See Google’s documentation:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • AdMob & privacy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services

In the European Economic Area (EEA), UK, and similar regions, a consent form (Google User Messaging Platform / UMP) may be shown before personalized ads are requested. You can change privacy choices where the platform provides a privacy options entry point.

4. Website analytics (Google Analytics)

Our websites on geeks0n-byte.github.io (including Spaceblox marketing pages, the browser play page, Shapes, and the studio home) use Google Analytics 4 to collect aggregated information about how the site is used, such as pages visited, approximate location derived from IP, device/browser type, and referral source. This helps us understand traffic and improve the site. Processing is performed by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. See also Google Analytics & privacy.

Where shown, a cookie / analytics choice banner lets you accept or reject Analytics and personalized ad storage (Google Consent Mode). Use the Cookies link in the page footer to change that choice later. You can also limit Analytics measurement with browser settings, extensions, or Google’s Analytics opt-out where available.

5. Children

Spaceblox is a general-audience puzzle game. It is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your country). If you believe a child has provided personal information through advertising services, contact us and we will help address it as appropriate.

6. Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Advertising and Analytics data may be processed by Google and advertising partners as part of serving ads and measuring the website. We do not share your local game saves with third parties.

7. Google Play Games cloud save (optional)

On Android, you may sign in to Google Play Games and sync campaign progress. When enabled, a JSON snapshot (progress, settings, achievements, and a timestamp) is stored in your Play Games account by Google—not on servers operated by us. Sync merges progress fields (for example, highest unlocked level and star bits) and may ask you to choose between device and cloud copies when timestamps tie. Mid-level session saves are not uploaded.

8. Data retention

  • Local game data: until you clear app data or uninstall.
  • Ad-related data: retained by Google / partners according to their policies.
  • Website Analytics: retained by Google according to the retention settings on our Analytics property.

9. Data deletion

Spaceblox does not create its own player accounts. To delete local game data associated with the App on your device:

  • Open Android Settings → Apps → Spaceblox → Storage → Clear storage / Clear data, or
  • Uninstall Spaceblox (this removes local saves on that device).

If you used optional Google Play Games sign-in, cloud progress is stored in your Google Play Games account. You can manage or delete that data through your Google account / Play Games settings, or contact us via the Spaceblox Google Play listing and we will help with a deletion request to the extent we can.

10. Your choices

  • Reset progress or clear app storage in Android system settings
  • Limit ad tracking in your device advertising / privacy settings
  • Use in-ad or UMP privacy options where available
  • Limit or opt out of Google Analytics in your browser (see section 4)
  • Uninstall the App at any time

11. International users

If you use the App or website outside your home country, advertising and Analytics partners may process data in other countries as described in Google’s policies.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the App or website after an update means you accept the revised policy. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.

13. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy: contact via the Google Play store listing for Spaceblox.
