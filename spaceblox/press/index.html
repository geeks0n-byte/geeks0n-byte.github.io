Short description

Spaceblox is a calm color-logic puzzle. Fill the grid with yellow, blue, green and purple tiles under a few strict rules: never three of a color in a row, keep yellow and blue balanced, respect = and × links, and slide purple tiles into place. Pure logic, and running out of time never ends a level.

Full description

Spaceblox is a calm color-logic puzzle made by Giga "gix0n" Sichinava. It is not a match-3.

Each level is a grid of empty cells. You fill it with colored tiles, and the finished board has to follow a few strict rules. You can never have three of the same color in a row. Yellow and blue have to stay balanced. Green counts as both colors at once for that three-in-a-row rule, which makes it useful and also easy to trip over.

As the campaign goes on, new ideas join in. Purple tiles fill a cell but, like green, do not count toward the yellow and blue balance. Some pairs of cells are linked: = means they must match and × means they must differ. Purple tiles hop between two linked cells: tap one to slide it along its arrow, unless another purple tile is blocking the way.

Every level has a solution you can reach with logic alone. There is no fail state, so you can take your time, undo and think. The campaign runs from easy to medium to hard.

Key features

  • Color-logic puzzles built on a few strict rules
  • Yellow, blue, green and purple tiles, = and × links, and purple tiles you slide into place
  • Levels are built to be solved with logic alone
  • No fail state: a time limit only affects an optional star
  • Campaign from easy to medium to hard
  • Free on Google Play, with a browser version
  • Achievements, colorblind mode and screen-reader labels. Google Play Games cloud save is in the Android app from version 1.1.0
  • Seven languages
