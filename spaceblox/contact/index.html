Contact

I am Giga Sichinava. On Google Play the name is gix0n. Spaceblox is my game, so a message about it comes to me. There is no support desk in front of this address.

Email: giga.sichinava@gmail.com

Spaceblox is free on Google Play and in the browser. If you are writing about a level, it helps me to know which of those you were using before you describe the bug. The rules and the star goals are in the guide if you need to point at one of them by name.

Bug reports

If something in Spaceblox does not do what the rules or the buttons say, I can act on a report that includes four things.

  • Where you played: the Android app, or the browser version.
  • The device. On Android, the model and the Android version. In a browser, which browser and which operating system.
  • The level number, or the name of the screen, where it happened. "Level select", "a purple hopper board", or "the pause menu" are all more useful than "in the game." The current public version is 1.1.0. If you are on the original Play upload, say v1.0, the 1 September 2026 launch.
  • What you expected, and what happened instead. A screenshot helps a lot, especially when the report is about a red highlight, a link, or a tile that looks out of place.

Version 1.1.0 on Android added Firebase Crashlytics, which sends me a crash report when the app actually crashes: device model, operating system version, and the state of the app at the time. That does not replace a letter. A crash report does not tell me what you were trying to do. If the game is wrong and still running, Crashlytics will not see it, and the email will.

Please do not send passwords, payment details, or other sensitive personal information. I do not need them to fix a puzzle bug, and email is the wrong place for them.

Feedback and puzzle ideas

A note that a level was too easy, or too hard, is useful. So is a note that a rule was confusing, or an idea for a new tile. I cannot promise that an idea becomes a tile. I can promise I will read it. The campaign is built so a level can be solved by reasoning, with automated checks on validation and on every campaign level. If you found a board where that seems false, I want the level and the placement that stuck you, not a general "this one is unfair." Say whether the level was easy, medium, or hard if you can tell, and whether you were on Android 1.1.0, on v1.0, or in the browser.

Translations

Spaceblox is in English, Spanish, German, French, Polish, Georgian, and Ukrainian. If you see a typo or an awkward phrase, send three things: the language, the screen, and the corrected text. "German, the pause menu, this button should say …" is enough for me to find the string. A screenshot of the bad line is a good addition. If a character is missing or a word is clipped on the phone, treat that as a bug report too: language, screen, and what you expected to read.

Messages in English are the easiest for me to answer quickly. A correction written in the language it corrects is still welcome. I would rather receive the right Polish sentence from you than a rough description of where it sits.

Privacy and data requests

Questions about the privacy policy, and requests to use a data-protection right such as access or deletion, should use the subject line Privacy request.

Most game data never reaches me. The app stores progress, stars, settings, and an in-progress level on your device. The browser stores them in that browser. You can delete the app data from the system settings for Spaceblox, by uninstalling, or with Reset profile in the game's Options. You can delete the browser save by clearing site data for geeks0n-byte.github.io. Optional Google Play Games cloud data is in your Google account, not on a server I run. In-progress levels are not uploaded. The policy explains those steps in full. If you write anyway, I will help as far as I can.

I do not need your save file attached to a first email. Say whether you are asking about the website, the Android app, or Play Games, and what you want done.

Press, creators, and streamers

You are welcome to stream Spaceblox or make a video about it. You do not need to ask first. The press kit has a ZIP with the logo, the app icon, key art, phone screenshots, and a short gameplay GIF. The page says you may use the text and those files for articles, videos, newsletters, and features about the game.

If you want a review copy, or you are writing for a named outlet or channel, email me and say which one. The press contact on the factsheet is the same address as the rest of this page. The factsheet also lists the public version. As of the live press page, that is 1.1.0 on Google Play, dated 5 October 2026. There is a trailer on YouTube if you need moving footage before the ZIP.

What to expect back

I read every message, and I reply as soon as I can. Spaceblox is made by one person, so I cannot promise a fixed response time. Some bug reports will not get an individual reply. They are still read, and they are still considered for a later update. If several people hit the same level, I may fix it in the next version instead of answering each letter.

If you are not sure which heading your note belongs under, send it anyway, to giga.sichinava@gmail.com, with as much of the detail above as you have. A short clear report is better than a complete one that never gets sent.
