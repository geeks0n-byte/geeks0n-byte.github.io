Contact

Spaceblox is made by Giga "gix0n" Sichinava, so messages go straight to the creator.

Email: giga.sichinava@gmail.com

What you can contact me about

Bug reports

If something in Spaceblox does not work as expected, please include:

  • where you played Spaceblox (Android app or browser);
  • your device model and Android version, or your browser and operating system;
  • the level number or screen where it happened;
  • what you expected and what happened instead — a screenshot helps a lot.

Feedback and puzzle ideas

Levels that felt too easy or too hard, rules that were confusing, or ideas for new tile types are genuinely useful.

Translations

Spaceblox is translated into seven languages (the list is under Features). If you spot a typo or an awkward phrase, tell me the language, the screen and the corrected text.

Privacy and data requests

For questions about the Privacy Policy, or to exercise your data-protection rights (for example access or deletion), write with the subject line "Privacy request". Most game data is stored only on your device, so the policy also explains how to delete it yourself.

Press, creators and streamers

You are welcome to stream or make videos of Spaceblox. For press questions, review copies or assets such as the icon and screenshots, get in touch and mention the outlet or channel.

What to expect

I read every message and reply as soon as I can. Spaceblox is made by one person, so I can't promise a fixed response time, and some bug reports may not get an individual reply, but every report is read and considered for future updates. Messages in English are best.

Please do not send passwords, payment details or other sensitive personal information by email.
