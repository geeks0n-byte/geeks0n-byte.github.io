Full-page ambient starfield for the Spaceblox look — calm parallax drift, soft twinkle, and sparse space FX. The main landing stays lighter; this page is optional.

Look behind the content: the entire viewport is a layered pixel space background. Parallax is intentionally slow so the scene feels like a gentle drift, not a rush.

Scroll the page: the starfield should keep drifting (GPU translate3d / rAF), not freeze the way CSS background-position often does on fixed layers in Chromium.

Motif density matches the in-game / landing banner feel (~720×1280 tiles, capped) — wide desktops may show gentle repeats rather than oversized stars.

Comets cycle fx_comet_1…3 at ~12 FPS on a long R→L cruise, matching space_background_fx.gd. Shooting stars stay steep streaks; asteroids spin.
