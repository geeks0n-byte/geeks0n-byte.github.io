HOW IT WORKS

  1. Fill empty cells with yellow, blue, or green.
  2. Never place three of the same color in a row or column.
  3. Keep the colors balanced on every line.
  4. Purple tiles add extra twists as you go.
  5. A full, valid board wins the level.
